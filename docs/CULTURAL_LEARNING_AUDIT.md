## 1. Grounded Pedagogical Framework

NULAR combines real-time 3D exploration with rigorous architectural ethnography. Every house experience provides:

1. **Geographic & Ethnic Attribution:**
   - Province, island group, administrative regency, and indigenous ethnic community (e.g. *Masyarakat Minangkabau Luhak Nan Tigo*, *Suku Dani / Hubula*, *Masyarakat Toraja Kete Kesu*).
2. **Cosmological & Cultural Philosophy:**
   - Traditional epistemologies explaining why buildings are constructed in specific geometries (e.g. Minangkabau *Alam Takambang Jadi Guru*, Javanese hierarchy of peace *Tentrem*, Torajan boat journey *Puya*).
3. **Tripartite Structural Systems:**
   - Ground substructure (*Kolong / Sulluk Banua / Umpak*).
   - Habitable body (*Badan Rumah / Kale Banua / Dalem*).
   - Sacred roof superstructure (*Gonjong / Longa / Tajug / Kerucut Niang*).
4. **Authentic Materiality:**
   - Documented traditional materials: seasoned teak (*Tectona grandis*), ironwood (*Eusideroxylon zwageri*), sago palm thatch, woven bamboo bedek, and unmortared river boulders.

## 2. Interactive Spatial Hotspots

Players can inspect architectural features in 3D by approaching floating beacons and pressing `[E]` (or tapping the mobile interaction button).

### Hotspot Example: Rumah Gadang
- **Atap Gonjong Melengkung:**
  - *Local Term:* Gonjong Tanduk Kabau
  - *Practical Function:* Rapidly sheds torrential tropical rainfall and facilitates natural convection chimney cooling.
  - *Cultural Meaning:* Represents water buffalo horns symbolizing victory, leadership, and harmony with nature.
- **Tiang Tuo & Batu Sandi:**
  - *Local Term:* Tiang Panjang Sandi
  - *Practical Function:* Non-rigid seismic dampening structure allowing the house to sway safely during high-magnitude earthquake tremors.
  - *Cultural Meaning:* Pillars of matrilineal unity binding extended families together.
- **Baanjuang (Raised Wing Dais):**
  - *Local Term:* Anjuang Tingkek
  - *Practical Function:* Elevated private chambers at the flanks of the communal hall.
  - *Cultural Meaning:* Dedicated status seating for clan matriarchs and bridal ceremonies.
- **Rangkiang Si Bayau-bayau:**
  - *Local Term:* Rangkiang Adat
  - *Practical Function:* Ventilated communal rice barn elevated on circular rat-guard timber posts.
  - *Cultural Meaning:* Safeguards agricultural prosperity and matrilineal food security during droughts.
