## 2. In-Depth Analysis: The 5 High-Priority Archetypes

### 2.1 Rumah Gadang (Minangkabau, Sumatera Barat)
- **Cultural Meaning:** Represents matrilineal kinship lineage, communal deliberative democracy (*ba'alua*), and cosmological harmony with nature (*alam takambang jadi guru*).
- **Iconic Architectural Features:**
  - *Gonjong:* Horn-like upward tapering gables reminiscent of water buffalo horns (*tanduk kabau*).
  - *Baanjuang:* Elevated end chambers at either flank reserved for maternal lineage heads or newly wedded brides.
  - *Tiang Tuo:* Central sacred pillars resting on unmortared flat river boulders (*batu umpak*) allowing dynamic seismic dampening.
  - *Ukiran Siriah Gadang & Pucuak Rabuang:* Intricate geometric floral friezes signifying fertility and growth.
- **Audit Finding:** The baseline procedural model possessed basic gonjong roofs, but lacked the characteristic concave swooping flaring curve, the articulated anjuang elevated platforms, and authentic wood carving panels.

### 2.2 Rumah Joglo (Jawa Tengah & DI Yogyakarta)
- **Cultural Meaning:** Emphasizes Javanese cosmological hierarchy, tranquility (*tentrem*), and spatial gradation from outward hospitality to inward ancestral sanctuary.
- **Iconic Architectural Features:**
  - *Pendopo:* Open colonnaded front pavilion for receiving guests and conducting gamelan or shadow puppet performances.
  - *Pringgitan:* Intermediate hallway connecting public realm to private living quarters.
  - *Dalem Ageng & Senthong:* Sacred inner sanctuary with central *Senthong Tengah* dedicated to Dewi Sri (goddess of rice and fertility).
  - *Soko Guru & Tumpang Sari:* Four massive central teak pillars crowned by multi-tiered stepped corbelled timber ceilings.
- **Audit Finding:** Walkable interior was present from Phase 2, but requires higher structural definition on the tumpang sari tiered carvings, distinct senthong partitioning, and authentic umpak pedestals.

### 2.3 Tongkonan (Toraja, Sulawesi Selatan)
- **Cultural Meaning:** Familial and spiritual anchor uniting descendants with ancestors (*aluk todolo*), symbolizing a cosmic boat journeying to the afterworld (*puya*).
- **Iconic Architectural Features:**
  - *Longa:* Dramatic sweeping cantilevered eaves projecting tens of meters outward at north and south facades.
  - *Tulak Somba & Kabongo':* Carved buffalo head sculptures and vertical rack holding real water buffalo horns (*tanduk kerbau*) marking familial status.
  - *Pa'ssura Carvings:* Black, red, yellow, and white natural pigments portraying sacred sun, chicken (*pa'tedong*), and fern spirals.
  - *Tripartite Division:* *Sulluk Banua* (underfloor stilt foundation), *Kale Banua* (living room divided into Tangdo, Sali, Sumbung), and *Ratiang Banua* (sacred attic).
- **Audit Finding:** Baseline model had a curved roof silhouette, but needed the distinctive forward boat rake, buffalo horn rack, and accurate tri-level floor elevation (Tangdo raised, Sali hearth, Sumbung rear).

### 2.4 Honai Pilamo Hubula (Papua Pegunungan - Dani / Hubula People)
- **Cultural Meaning:** Communal men's assembly and council house (*pilamo*), designed for thermal warmth in chilly alpine highlands (1,600m - 2,500m ASL).
- **Iconic Architectural Features:**
  - *Atap Alang-Alang Membulat:* Conical dome thatched with thick woven wild grass (*alang-alang*) shedding rainfall smoothly without eaves.
  - *Double-Deck Interior:* Lower level (*lantai bawah*) featuring compacted earth with a sunken central hearth (*wul-wul* / *lokop*); upper mezzanine floor (*lantai atas*) woven from wooden boards and dried grass for communal sleeping.
  - *Pintu Rendah:* Deliberately low entrance (under 1.2m) requiring entering visitors to stoop in humility and trapping warmth inside.
- **Audit Finding:** Baseline model had circular geometry, but lacked the internal second-tier mezzanine sleeping deck, interior central hearth stones, and narrow stooping entryway.

### 2.5 Mbaru Niang (Wae Rebo, Manggarai, NTT)
- **Cultural Meaning:** Conical ancestral dwelling embodying Manggarai cosmotheandric philosophy — unity between humans, ancestral spirits (*mori kraeng*), and the earth.
- **Iconic Architectural Features:**
  - *5 Concentric Levels:*
    1. *Tenda / Lutur:* Ground floor living quarters for families.
    2. *Lobo:* Second tier for storing food reserves and household goods.
    3. *Lentar:* Third tier for preserving seed corn and agricultural grains for next harvest.
    4. *Lempa Rae:* Fourth tier for drought contingency food stocks.
    5. *Hekang Kode:* Sacred pinnacle tier under the roof cone reserved for ancestral offerings.
  - *Siri Ngando / Hiri Bongkok:* Massive central wooden tree trunk acting as cosmological axis mundi.
- **Audit Finding:** Baseline model had conical shape, but was single-layer internally. Requires internal tiered ring beams (*rede*), central *siri ngando* pillar, and distinctive roof apex finial.
