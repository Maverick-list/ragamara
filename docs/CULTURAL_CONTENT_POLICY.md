# NULAR — Cultural Content Policy

1. **Zero Fabrication:** Never invent historical dates, ethnic attributions, architectural dimensions, or local language terms. Mark unverified fields transparently as *"Dalam penelitian / Requires verification"*.
2. **Tangible vs. Intangible Heritage:** Physical buildings and artifacts are classified as *tangible heritage*; carpentry knowledge (*Kawruh Kalang*), weaving/batik techniques, dances, and ceremonies are attributed precisely as *intangible heritage* (DAPOBUD / UNESCO ICH).
3. **Textile Accuracy:** Never label non-batik textiles (*Tenun Ikat*, *Songket*, *Ulos*, *Tapis*, *Sasirangan*) as Batik. Preserve exact regional origins and weaving/dyeing classifications.
4. **Traditional Weapons Policy:** Weapons (*Rencong*, *Keris*, *Mandau*) are presented strictly as historical and cultural museum artifacts. Combat mechanics, violence, or real-world weapon crafting tutorials are strictly prohibited.
5. **Fictional Framing Separation:** Story-driven framing (*"The Nusantara Heritage Journey"*) is clearly labeled as an educational narrative wrapper and never presented as historical fact.
6. **Localization Integrity:** Original Indonesian/regional artifact names are always preserved alongside translations. Machine-assisted or community-drafted regional translations are labeled clearly as unverified.
