# Phase 1 — Cultural Knowledge System & Indonesia Living Atlas (38 Provinces)

## Deliverables Completed
1. **Schema v3.0.0 Cultural Database (`data/houses.json`):** Expanded from 14 houses (10 provinces) to **42 verified traditional houses covering all 38 Indonesian provinces**, preserving multi-tradition provinces (Sumatera Barat: Rumah Gadang + Uma Mentawai; Sumatera Utara: Rumah Bolon + Siwaluh Jabu; Sulawesi Selatan: Tongkonan + Saoraja; Jawa Tengah: Joglo + Limasan) and integrating every entry from `Deskripsi_Rumah_Adat.docx` (35 entries), `Ringkasan Sejarah & Bagian Rumah Adat Osing.docx` (Jawa Timur), `Pekarangan Tradisional Bali` (Bali), and `Rumah ndalem` (DI Yogyakarta).
2. **10-Section Research Monographs (`house.html`):** Every house now renders a structured 10-section architectural research article sourced from the Drive documents and academic literature.
3. **Authoritative 38-Province GeoJSON Living Atlas (`map.html` + `js/atlas-map.js` + `data/indonesia-38-provinces.geojson`):** Simplified MIT-licensed GeoJSON boundaries for all 38 provinces (including the 2022 Papua divisions), island zoom controls, search, architectural typology filters, clickable province polygons, multi-house provincial dossiers, and discovery progress.
4. **Nusantara Textile Provenance Registry (`data/textiles.json`):** Accurately separates Batik (Parang, Megamendung) from non-batik textiles (Tenun Ikat Sumba, Songket Pandai Sikek, Ulos Batak, Sasirangan Banjar).
5. **Source & Attribution Registry (`data/source-registry.json` & `data/photo-attributions.json`):** Full attribution for all 42 houses, 27 optimized Drive exterior reference renders, 14 existing Wikimedia photos, and 1 new Wikimedia photo for Rumah Adat Osing (CC BY-SA 4.0, Bachtiar Djanan).
6. **Automated Data & Browser Verification (`tests/verify_phase1_atlas.mjs`):** Validates all 38 provinces, GeoJSON integrity, 10-section articles, 42 house cards with 0 broken images, responsive mobile layout (390×844), and 0 console/HTTP errors.
